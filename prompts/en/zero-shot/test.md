# Idea Generator Prompt

**Category:** Creativity  
**Use Case:** Brainstorming product or business ideas

---

**Prompt:**

> You are a creative brainstorming assistant. Generate 5 innovative business ideas in the tech industry based on the following keywords:
> 
> - Keyword 1: {{keyword1}}
> - Keyword 2: {{keyword2}}
> - Keyword 3: {{keyword3}}

---

**Instructions:**
Replace `{{keyword1}}`, `{{keyword2}}`, and `{{keyword3}}` with your chosen topics. Ask ChatGPT to expand on the most promising idea.

---

**Example Output:**

1. AI-powered career coach for remote workers  
2. Micro-SaaS for freelancers managing client contracts  
...
